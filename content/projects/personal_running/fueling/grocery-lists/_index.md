# Grocery Lists

Reusable grocery templates and weekly shopping lists for training.

## Grocery Backbone

A default list of staples that can support most training weeks.

- Rice
- Pasta or orzo
- Oats
- Beans or lentils
- Eggs
- Yogurt
- Chicken, tofu, or ground beef
- Frozen fruit
- Fresh vegetables
- Potatoes or sweet potatoes
- Bread or tortillas
- Electrolytes or hydration mix if needed
