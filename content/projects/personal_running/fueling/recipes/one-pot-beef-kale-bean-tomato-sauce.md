# One-Pot Beef, Kale, Bean, Tomato Sauce

A permanent dinner anchor for summer training.

## Use Case

Cheap, flexible, high-protein, high-fiber dinner that can be eaten with pasta, rice, bread, or tortillas.

## Ingredients

- Ground beef
- Kale
- Beans
- Tomato sauce
- Onion
- Garlic
- Olive oil
- Salt and seasonings
- Pasta, rice, bread, or tortillas for serving

## Grocery Notes

- Works well as a batch-cook meal.
- Freezes well.
- Can be stretched with extra beans, lentils, vegetables, or pasta.

## Modifications

- Higher-carb version: serve with pasta or rice.
- Higher-protein version: add more beef or beans.
- Budget version: use more beans/lentils and less beef.
- Vegetarian version: replace beef with lentils or beans.
