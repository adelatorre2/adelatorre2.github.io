# Summer 2026 Training Meal Plan

A flexible grocery backbone and meal system for summer base building.

## Related Training Log

[Summer 2026 Training Log](/projects/personal_running/summer26_training/)

## Guiding Idea

This is not a rigid meal plan.

The goal is to keep a small set of reliable foods in the apartment so that most meals are easy, cheap, nutritious, and good enough to repeat. That gives me structure without turning food into another source of stress.

The working rule:

> Get most of my calories from a few nutritious defaults, then leave room for social meals, restaurants, experiments, and cravings.

For this training block, the priority is not active weight loss. The priority is to rebuild consistency, run more, lift consistently, eat enough carbohydrates, hit protein regularly, and let my body settle as training becomes more stable.

## Core Grocery List

### Breakfast Staples

Always try to have:

- Rolled oats
- Greek yogurt
- Milk
- Protein powder
- Frozen berries
- Bananas
- Peanut butter
- Honey
- Chia seeds
- Coffee beans

Default meals from this category:

- Overnight oats
- Hot oatmeal
- Smoothies
- Yogurt bowls
- PB banana toast
- Protein shakes

### Carbs

Always try to have:

- Rice
- Pasta
- Bread
- Tortillas
- Potatoes
- Bananas
- Oats

Notes:

Carbs are training fuel. As mileage climbs, especially toward 60–80 mpw, this category becomes more important, not less.

### Proteins

Always try to have:

- Chicken thighs
- Ground beef
- Eggs
- Greek yogurt
- Black beans
- Lentils
- Protein powder
- Tuna
- Tofu, when useful

Notes:

These are affordable, flexible, and easy to build meals around.

### Vegetables

Always try to have:

- Onions
- Bell peppers
- Spinach
- Kale
- Frozen mixed vegetables
- Broccoli
- Garlic

Notes:

These are the backbone for pasta sauces, rice bowls, stir-fries, breakfast hashes, and soups.

### Flavor

Always try to have:

- Salsa
- Hot sauce
- Soy sauce
- Pesto
- Garlic
- Limes
- Olive oil
- Tomato sauce
- Seasonings

Notes:

Food has to taste good. Flavor is what makes repeatable meals sustainable.

## Training Load Adjustments

### Lower-mileage or lighter weeks

Keep the same foods, but use smaller portions:

- Normal oat bowl or smoothie
- Regular rice bowl
- Normal pasta or stir-fry dinner
- Snacks only as needed

### Higher-mileage weeks

Scale portions up:

- More oats or milk at breakfast
- Extra rice, pasta, tortillas, potatoes, or bread
- Add snacks around training
- Add electrolytes when heat or sweat loss is higher
- Add an extra protein serving when lifting more consistently

### Long-run days

Prioritize:

- Carb-forward dinner the night before
- Simple pre-run snack if needed
- Post-run breakfast with carbs and protein
- Hydration and electrolytes if hot

## Simple Meal Formula

Most meals should follow:

Carb + protein + vegetable + flavor

Examples:

- Rice + chicken + peppers/onions + salsa
- Pasta + beef/lentils + kale/spinach + tomato sauce
- Tortillas + eggs/beans/chicken + vegetables + hot sauce
- Oats + yogurt/protein + banana/berries + peanut butter
