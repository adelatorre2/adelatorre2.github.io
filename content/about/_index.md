# About

Hi — I’m Alejandro De La Torre. I’m an alumnus of the [**University of Wisconsin–Madison**](https://www.wisc.edu/), C/O '26, where I earned a B.A. in [**Economics**](https://econ.wisc.edu/) (Mathematical Emphasis), a B.A. in [**Information Science**](https://ischool.wisc.edu/), and a Certificate in [**Mathematics**](https://math.wisc.edu/). I’m now an Analyst at [**Analysis Group**](https://www.analysisgroup.com/) in **Washington, D.C.**

I’m early in my career, but I think I want to do econ research. For now, my focus is simple: stay curious, be a supportive team member, and live-laugh-love through it all.

*Views are my own.*
